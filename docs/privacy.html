Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BlueChip Rewards ("we", "us", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our Android application and related services (collectively, the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

Account information

  • When you register with email: your email address, a display name, and an encrypted password (we never store your password in plain text).
  • When you sign in with Google: your name, email address, profile picture, and Google account identifier, as provided by Google Sign-In. We use this only to create and authenticate your account.
  • Profile details you choose to add, such as a display name or avatar.

Activity and reward data

  • Your in-app activity used to calculate rewards — daily check-ins, mining sessions, scratch cards, quiz attempts, tasks, referrals — and the resulting BlueChip Points (BCP) ledger and wallet balance.
  • Referral relationships (which account referred you, or which accounts you referred).

Withdrawal information

  • Payment details you voluntarily provide to request a withdrawal (for example, a UPI ID, wallet address, or account details). We use these solely to review and process your withdrawal request.

Device, log, and advertising data

  • Basic technical data needed to operate and secure the Service, such as app version and general device information.
  • Advertising identifiers processed by Google AdMob when ads are shown (see "Advertising" below).

2. How we use your information

  • To create your account and authenticate you.
  • To operate the earning features and maintain an accurate, auditable record of your BCP balance.
  • To detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, and violations of our Terms of Service.
  • To review and process manual withdrawal requests.
  • To send you service-related notifications (for example, reward confirmations and withdrawal status updates).
  • To display advertising and reward you for watching rewarded ads.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Google user data

If you sign in with Google, we access your basic Google profile (name, email address, profile picture, and Google account ID) only to create and secure your BlueChip Rewards account. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We do not sell your Google user data, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Advertising

The app shows advertising through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use device identifiers and related data in accordance with their own policies. You can learn more and manage choices via Google's Advertising policies. We reward you only after a valid, completed rewarded-ad event; reward amounts are determined on our server, not by the app.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the Service:

  • Supabase — our backend, database, and authentication provider, which stores your account and reward data on our behalf.
  • Google — for Google Sign-In and for AdMob advertising.
  • Legal and safety — where required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and integrity of the Service and its users.

6. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service, maintain an accurate reward ledger, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data except where we must retain certain records (for example, for fraud prevention or legal compliance).

7. Security

We take security seriously. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords are stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider, and all balance and reward changes are enforced on the server behind row-level security so the app can never alter balances directly. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information using industry-standard safeguards.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction: you can view and update your profile in the app.
  • Deletion: you may request deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting us (see "Contact").
  • Notifications: you can manage notification preferences on your device.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA; contact us to exercise them.

9. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. International users

Your information may be processed and stored in countries other than your own, where data-protection laws may differ. By using the Service you consent to such processing.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective date" above and, where appropriate, through in-app notice. Your continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
